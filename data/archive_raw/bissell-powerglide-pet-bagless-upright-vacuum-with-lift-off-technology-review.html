Features of the BISSELL PowerGlide Pet Bagless Upright Vacuum with Lift-Off Technology

BISSELL PowerGlide Pet Bagless Upright Vacuum with Lift-Off Technology is designed to meet the needs of the consumers. After hours of research, testing and analyzing the features, we put BISSELL PowerGlide is a one of the best in our Upright Vacuum cleaner List.

Multi-Level Filtration

Quality filtration is mandatory in every vacuum cleaner, especially if you are sensitive to allergens. No one wants to stay in house where dust clogs the air, hence the need for a filtration system in your cleaning appliances. BISSELL offers multi-filtration in this vacuum cleaner, which helps to decrease allergens in the air. This cleanup power is very impressive for this compact cleaner.

Lift-Off Technology

The most prominent feature of the BISSELL PowerGlide is its Lift-Off technology, which allows you to clean without restriction. So how exactly does this technology work? Well, when you are cleaning whole rooms you can use the PowerGlide as a full-size upright vacuum.

However, when you want to clean the stairs and other hard to reach areas, you can convert the PowerGlide into an easy-release portable vacuum cleaner for convenience. This means that you can clean wherever you want and whenever you want without restriction.

No Loss Suction

There are many vacuum cleaners on the market that have fading suction, due to dirt accumulating in the filters and clogging it. This is incredibly inconvenient, as it means that your cleaning becomes less efficient then it was before.

That is why BISSELL uses multi-cyclonic separation, which maintains powerful suction by holding huge debris in your tank as opposed to your filters.

Versatile Cleaning

The BISSELL PowerGlide comes with a brush roll switch, which allows you to clean multiple surfaces without any trouble. This means that you can collect dirt, dust, and debris from hard floors without creating a mess.

Additionally, a Pet TurboEraser Tool allows you to lift hair and dirt from upholstery and dirt, using powerful bristles that rotate with force and efficiency.

Easy to Maneuver

No one likes an upright vacuum cleaner that is difficult to maneuver, and that is why BISSELL has incorporated swivel steering into the PowerGlide. This steering allows you to navigate around furniture and obstacles with incredible ease, for trouble free cleaning.
