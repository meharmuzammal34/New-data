Eureka 3670G Mighty Mite Canister Vacuum Review

Eureka has made a great name for itself when it comes to home appliances, and the 3670G happens to be one of its most popular creations. This sleek canister vacuum has been designed to make your floor cleaning tasks easier and more enjoyable, using a lightweight design and convenient features. While the vacuum has been designed to clean wood floors, bare floors and tile, it can also execute excellent vacuuming on your carpets and rugs.

This highly maneuverable canister vacuum will suck up dust and pet hair without any hassle, leaving your floors, stairs, upholstery, and car amazingly clean. So if you are looking for a well built and well designed vacuum cleaner that will give you value for your money, read on for a comprehensive review of the Eureka 3670G Mighty Mite canister vacuum.

Great Performance

When you buy a vacuum cleaner you need to be sure that it can perform at amazing levels. Thankfully, the Mighty Mite offers high-performance, starting with its 12 amp motor that crates powerful suction so that your cleaning tasks can be quick and efficient. This motor allows you to pick up dust, dirt, and pet hair with ease, so that your home and your car can be free of any dirt particles.

Additionally, the Mighty Mite canister vacuum has a triple filtration bag system, which works to eliminate allergens from your home. In this way the air can be kept fresh during and after cleaning. Last but not least, the canister vacuum has a 2-setting height adjustment feature that allows you to seamlessly transition from bare floors to rugs and vice versa.

Convenient Cleaning

Another great benefit of the Mighty Mite is its convenience, and you can see this through the on-board tools that it comes with. These tools include a crevice tool, a carpet/bare floor nozzle, 2 extension wands, and an upholstery nozzle/dusting brush. These convenient tools allow you to reach tricky areas, such as ceilings, drapes, stairs, car interiors, and upholstery.

The canister vacuum is also outfitted with efficient brush rolls, which deep clean your carpets. The lightweight and portable vacuum cleaner is therefore perfect for multi-surface cleaning.

Blower Port and Dust Bag

Another great feature of the Eureka Mighty Mite is its blower port, which works by reversing airflow so that dirt and debris can be blown away. This blower port can be used for blowing sawdust in workshops, dirt in garage spaces, and dust off keyboards.

The bagged vacuum cleaner also has a dust bag, which is easy to remove and empty for a no-mess cleanup process.

Maneuverability

Maneuverability is also important in a canister vacuum, and it is for this reason that Eureka has designed the 3670G to be easy to move. This vacuum cleaner is composed of a compact, portable, and lightweight design, which makes it easy to carry around the house.

The large wheels are also easy to roll, meaning that you can maneuver it between rooms and on the stairs as you go about your vacuuming.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. What tools can you find in the six-piece attachment set of the Eureka 3670G?

A. The 3670G attachment set includes 2 extension wands, a seven foot crush-proof hose, an upholstery nozzle, a carpet/bare floor nozzle, and a crevice tool.

Q. Does the 12 amp Mighty Mite run too hot when you use it continuously?

A. No. The Eureka 3670G vacuum will not overheat, and you can use it continuously without worrying about this problem. It also has an overheat feature which automatically turns off the vacuum cleaner in case the motor gets too hot.

Final Verdict

As you can see, the Mighty Mite canister vacuum excels in the areas of maneuverability, power, convenience and ease of use. So if you are looking for a vacuum cleaner that works magnificently on hardwood floors and short carpets, you should definitely go with the Eureka Mighty Mite.

This canister vacuum will not let you down!
