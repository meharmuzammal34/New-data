Hoover Linx Cordless Stick Vacuum Cleaner Review

Hoover is synonymous with vacuum cleaning. For over 100 years this company has introduced one appliance after another, all dedicated to making the cleaning process easier and more enjoyable. The Linx Cordless in particular is a stick vacuum that aims to transform how you clean on a daily basis, with an ergonomic design and a versatile set of features that will leave you in awe.

Whether you want to clean your bare floors or wall-to-wall carpets, or pick up crumbs or pet hair, this vacuum cleaner is perfect for you. The easy to use vacuum cleaner will take care of your entire floor to ceiling cleaning, without needing you to plug into an outlet.

So if you are looking for a sleek and versatile cleaning tool that is packed with advanced technology and smart features, read the Hoover Link Cordless vacuum cleaner review below.

WindTunnel Technology

The WindTunnel technology found in this stick vacuum cleaner uses a unique air flow system to lift up dirt found deep in your hard floors and carpets. This technology is composed of 3 tunnels which will remove all the dirt that hides itself in your carpet fibers, and thereafter it will hold this dirt in the vacuum as opposed to scattering it back on your floor.

Intuitive Controls

Another great aspect of the Hoover Linx is its intuitive controls, which make it much easier to use. The power switch of the vacuum cleaner is conveniently positioned at the top of the handle, which is within easy reach of your fingertips. Additionally, the Linx has a light indicator with labels for suction-only and suction-and-brushroll, so that you know which cleaning mode you are on. You can also alternate between these two modes with the flip of a switch.

Furthermore, the vacuum cleaner has a convenient LED status bar that lets you know when to recharge the battery.

Excellent Performance

The Hoover Linx delivers amazing performance, thanks to its edge cleaning bristles and wide cleaning path. The bristles bring you closer to the walls and corners for edge-to-edge cleaning, while the wide cleaning path will cover more surface area for a quick cleaning time.

The Linx also has cyclonic filtration which prevents your filters form clogging and your suction power from waning. Lastly, this stick vacuum cleaner which is powered by an interchangeable lithium-ion battery has 3 convenient cleaning modes, including off, suction only (for hardwood floors and bare floors), and suction-and-brushroll (for carpets).

Ease of Use

Another great aspect of the Hoover Linx is its ease of use, which comes as a result of its smart design. The cordless vacuum has a low profile base and extreme recline handle, which work to ensure that you can reach far under counter edges and furniture with ease.

Furthermore, the light and compact upright vacuum cleaner has replaceable and washable filters, and a bottom release dirt cup. This dirt cup allows you to clean up the vacuum without creating a mess.

Battery

The Linx Stick vacuum is a cordless cleaner, which means that you can move from room to room without the restriction of a cord. The Linx uses a clever battery-powered design with a small charger, and you can remove the battery and charge it when required.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Will this vacuum cleaner work well on hardwood floors?

A. Yes, the Hoover Linx works well on hardwood floors without any scratching. You should keep it on the first setting to prevent the brush from turning.

Q. Can I leave the battery in the charger?

A. Hoover has said that storing the battery in the charger can cause it to fail. You should therefore not leave the battery in the charger for more than a few hours.

Final Verdict

The Hoover Linx might look like the standard stick vacuum, but its incredible design, usability, and performance makes it stand out from similar options on the market. So if you are looking for a classic and intuitive vacuum cleaner that will not burn a hole in your pocket, I recommend the Linx without any hesitation.
