Features of the Kenmore Kenmore Canister Vacuum Cleaner, Progressive, Blueberry 21614

Kenmore Kenmore Canister Vacuum Cleaner, Progressive, Blueberry 21614 is designed to meet the needs of the consumers. After hours of research, testing and analyzing the features, we put Kenmore Kenmore Progressive Canister is a one of the best in our Canister Vacuum Cleaner List.

Smart Handle

Kenmore has decided to integrate a Crossover Handle into the Progressive Canister vacuum, and this handle can be used to store your crevice tools and dusting brush. This is quite convenient for you, and it allows you to access your tools with ease.

Furthermore, the handle allows you to transition from carpets to bare floors without any hassle.

Easy Stair and Above-Floor Cleaning

Kenmore has also incorporated a Stair Grip feature into the vacuum, so that the vacuum cleaner can rest while on the staircase. This exclusive feature makes your stair cleaning much easier.

Furthermore, the vacuum cleaner comes with a telescopic wand as well as a swiveling 7-foot hose, so that you can carry out your above-floor cleaning with ease.

Convenient Attachments

When it comes to attachments, you will be happy with what Kenmore canister has to offer you. You have a Pet Upholstery Tool and Pet PowerMate for cleaning pet hair from your furniture, and a Horsehair dusting brush for cleaning floors and fine wooden furniture. These attachments allow you to clean every surface of your home with incredible ease.

HEPA Filtration

I remember buying my first vacuum cleaner, which expelled dust back into the air as I went about my cleaning. I then had to air out my house to stop the coughing and sneezing brought about by the dust.

Thankfully Kenmore has used a HEPA filtration system in the Progressive Canister vacuum cleaner, so that dust and pet dander can be kept out of the air. The cleaner air is much easier to breathe.

Variable Power

Last but not least, the Kenmore Canister Vacuum Cleaner boasts of variable power settings. You can now adjust the speed of the vacuum depending on how delicate the surface is, and you can adjust the power of the vacuum depending on how much dirt and dust you need to pick up.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Does the vacuum cleaner come with a switch to turn off the rotating brushes?

A. Yes, the vacuum cleaner has a switch located on the handle which allows you to control the rotating brushes. You can engage the brushes when you are cleaning the carpets, and turn them off when you are cleaning bare floors.

Q. How much does the vacuum cleaner weigh?

A. The vacuum cleaner weighs 23lbs, which is not very light. However, it will not weigh you down and leave you fatigued either.
