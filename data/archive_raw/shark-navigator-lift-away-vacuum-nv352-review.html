Features of the Shark Navigator Lift-Away Vacuum (NV352)

Shark Navigator Lift-Away Vacuum (NV352) is designed to meet the needs of the consumers. After hours of research, testing and analyzing the features, we put Shark Navigator is a one of the best in our Vacuum cleaner List.

Great Suction

The most prominent feature of the Shark Navigator is its Never Lose Suction Technology, which manages to remove dirt from fine air so that it does not clog your filters. When dirt clogs your filters then your suction power will begin to wane as you go about your cleaning, and that is what this technology seeks to prevent. All in all, the Shark Navigator NV352 will maintain a strong suction power all day long.

Anti-Allergen

The Shark NV352 comes with anti-allergen complete seal technology, which works seamlessly with HEPA filters. The result is a vacuum cleaner that can capture 99.9% of dust and allergens, and hold it in the vacuum so that the air in your home is easy to breathe.

Easy to Operate

The point of buying a vacuum cleaner is to make your cleaning process much easier. It therefore makes no sense to buy a vacuum that you will struggle to move from room to room. Thankfully, the NV352 is a lightweight appliance that is incredibly easy to maneuver. Furthermore, it comes with swivel steering so that you can move around furniture and obstacles with ease.

The convenient vacuum is therefore great for cleaning upholstery, stairs, small spaces, and other hard to reach areas on your house.

Versatile

Lastly, the Shark Navigator comes with versatile attachments, including 2 crevice tools, a dusting brush, and a small pet hair power brush. These will help you lift dirt and pet hair from around your house.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. What is the difference between the Shark NV352 and NV356?

A. Firstly, the NV352 comes with a 25ft cord, while the NV356 comes with a 30ft cord. Additionally, the hard floor tools in each model are different, and the NV356 comes with a microfiber dust pad for the hardwood floor attachment. The 356 is a professional vacuum, and it therefore has a larger dust cup and a heavier weight. The NV356 also has a bag where you can store your tools.

Q. How do you clean the filter?

A. Simply take out the HEPA filter at the front of the vacuum and wash it. Then lift out the canister at the base of the vacuum, and remove the foam filters located there. Wash these foam filters like you did with the HEPA filter.

Q. Is it difficult to put the vacuum cleaner together?

A. No, it is very simple to put the Shark Navigator NV352 together.
