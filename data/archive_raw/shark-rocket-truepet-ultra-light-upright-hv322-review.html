If you have cats and dogs at home, then you know the daily struggle of picking up the pet hair and dander that they leave around. As a pet lover it might not be easy to leave your dog outside, and therefore the best option would be to find an efficient way to keep your house clean. One option that works magnificently for me is using a pet-specific vacuum cleaner, which will efficiently pick up anything your pet leaves behind.

The Shark Rocket TruePet HV322 in particular has proven to be one of the most popular and effective upright cleaners available. This vacuum cleaner does not lose power or suction, ensuring that your cleaning performance is amazing. This portable, lightweight and maneuverable vacuum will clean every surface from your floor to your ceiling, so that cleaning up after your pet is no longer a headache.

Below we will review the HV322 in greater detail, so that you can know everything about this vacuum.

Who is it made for?

The Shark Rocket HV322 is designed for people with pets; however, it will also work well for pet-free homes. This vacuum cleaner can clean the bare floors, carpets, furniture, stairs, and tight spaces in your home.

Features of the Shark Rocket TruePet Ultra-Light Upright (HV322)

Shark Rocket TruePet Ultra-Light Upright (HV322) is designed to meet the needs of the consumers. After hours of research, testing and analyzing the features, we put Shark Rocket TruePet is a one of the best in our Upright vacuum cleaner List.

Never Lose Suction

One of my favorite features of the Shark Rocket TruePet is its powerful suction and power, which will be maintained throughout your cleaning. This upright vacuum cleaner will deep clean your carpets and your bare floors better than any other cleaners you have ever used. The corded cleaner also makes use of cyclonic technology, which generates the force that you need to separate fine dirt from the airflow. In this way the filters will never be clogged with dirt, and your vacuum cleaner will never lose suction.

All of this power comes in a compact and lightweight vacuum cleaner, which is convenient to move around the house.

Quality Dust Cup Construction

The ultra-lightweight vacuum cleaner comes with a large dust cup, which has two times the capacity of the original Rocket. This dust cup is also easy to empty, so that you can clean out your vacuum cleaner when it fills up.

Easy to Handle

The Shark Rocket also comes with advanced swivel steering, which allows you to maneuver the vacuum cleaner with ease. This vacuum cleaner also includes a quick release foot pedal, so that you can move from the floor to the ceiling without hassle.

The vacuum cleaner also has a wand that you can use to reach narrow spaces and high areas, and you can use it as a handheld when you want to clean furniture and stairs. The slim vacuum cleaner also has LED lights for illumination as you clean.

Versatile Cleaning

The Shark Rocket comes with a ton of attachments, which you can use for versatility. These attachments include a TruePet motorized brush, a hard floor genie, a crevice tool, a dusting brush, a pet hair tool, and 2 speed brushes roll. These tools fit neatly in your on-board storage clip, for convenience and easy access.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. What is the difference between the Shark Rocket TruePet Ultra-Light and the Shark Rocket Deluxe Pro Ultra-Light?

A. There are few discernible differences between these two vacuum cleaners. However, the TruePet has more accessories than the Deluxe Pro.

Q. Is it easy to empty the dust cup?

A. Yes, it is incredibly easy to empty the dust cup. All you need to do is press the release button, and the dust cup will pull right out. Once you tilt the dust cup, all the pet hair that you have collected will fall straight into your bin.
