Features of the Shark Rotator NV752 Powered Lift-Away TruePet Vacuum, Bordeaux

Shark Rotator NV752 Powered Lift-Away TruePet Vacuum, Bordeaux is designed to meet the needs of the consumers. After hours of research, testing and analyzing the features, we put Shark Rotator NV752 is a one of the best in our vacuum cleaner List.

Versatile Performance

Versatility is important in your vacuum cleaner, and thankfully the Shark Rotator NV752 offers this. This vacuum cleaner is essentially 3 vacuums in one, as it can perform as an upright, a lift-away pod, and a canister vacuum.

When you use it as an upright you can be assured of power, when you use it as a lift-away pod you can deep clean your carpets and upholstery, and when you use it as a portable canister you can effortlessly clean different parts of your home.

Quality Attachments

One of the ways in which the Shark Rotator NV752 stands out from the crowd is through its quality attachments. This powerful vacuum comes with numerous attachments for different purposes, such as a hard-floor genie attachment, a motorized brush roll, and a TruePet mini-motorized brush attachment.

As you can imagine, the genie attachment is excellent for bare floor cleaning, the motorized brush roll is perfect for deep cleaning carpets and under furniture, and the TruePet brush is great for picking up pet hair and dander. These attachments will ensure that even the finest debris will be picked up from all surfaces.

Excellent Suction and Filtration

The NV752 has been designed to maintain suction power throughout your cleaning, and it does this by preventing dirt from clogging your filters. Furthermore, the Shark Rotator comes with anti-allergen complete seal technology, which works with HEPA filters. These filters will trap up to 99.99% of allergens and dust, so that your air remains clean.

LED Lights and Convenient Controls

One of the most interesting features of the Shark Rotator NV752 is its LED lights, which are located on the wand for cleaning drapes and ceilings. Furthermore, the vacuum cleaner has an illuminated handle, ensuring that you can clearly see hidden debris as you go about you cleaning. By lighting up your work area the LED lights ensure that everything is visible to you.

There are also controls located at your fingertips, so that you can transition from carpet to floors with ease.
