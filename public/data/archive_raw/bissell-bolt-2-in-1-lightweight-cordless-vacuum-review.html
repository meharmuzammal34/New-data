BISSELL BOLT 2-in-1 Lightweight Cordless Vacuum Features

BISSELL BOLT 2-in-1 Lightweight Cordless Vacuum is designed to meet the needs of the consumers. After hours of research, testing and analyzing the features, we put BISSELL BOLT 2-in-1 is a one of the best in our Cordless Vacuum List.

Removable Hand Vacuum

Dealing with small messes inside the house is effectively achieved with the removable hand vacuum. Imagine a scenario where your cat pukes on the carpet or your child stains the floor or carpet; you can easily remove the hand vacuum in order to deal with such small messes or reach above-floor surfaces.

Powerful 12 V Battery

BISSELL BOLT 2-in-1 vacuum cleaner does not lose its suction. With its 12 V battery, you can clean the entire room and get more dirt. This will leave all the surfaces clean and healthy for you and your family.

2-Way Folding Handle

If you have a relatively smaller house, storage space for your cordless vacuum may be a difficult task. You do not have to worry if you do not have enough space; you can fold the handle forward or backward to clean under the furniture and as well for a convenient storage.

Lightweight Cordless Vacuum

One of the greatest benefits of BISSELL BOLT 2-in-1 vacuum is that it is cordless. You can move with it to any place you require to do clean. Its lightweight design makes it easy to carry the cleaner on the stairs and other rooms in your house that needs to be cleaned.

Edge Bristles

Reaching corners and other edges in your house can be a difficult task if your vacuum doesn’t have side bristles. BISSELL ensures that their products meet all your cleaning needs. This vacuum’s side bristles makes it a simple task to sweep away debris from hard to reach areas.
