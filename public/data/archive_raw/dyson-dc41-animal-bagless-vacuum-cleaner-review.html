Dyson DC41 Animal Bagless Vacuum Cleaner Review

When you think of Dyson, the first thing that probably comes to your mind is ‘amazing design’ and ‘excellent workmanship’. And you wouldn’t be wrong; Dyson has managed to dominate the home appliance market with its amazing vacuum cleaner creations.

The DC41 is the embodiment of Dyson, offering all it users a modern, sturdy, sophisticated, and high performing vacuum cleaner. At $649, this vacuum is the most expensive vacuum cleaner model Dyson has to offer.

So what can you expect for all of this cash?

Well, the Dyson DC41 Animal Upright Vacuum provides you with all the tools that you need to eliminate dust, dirt, allergens, and pet hair from your house.

Features of the Dyson DC41 Animal Bagless Vacuum Cleaner

With advanced Dyson technology and craftsmanship, the Dyson DC41 solidified its spot as one of the best vacuum cleaners on the market. So if you are interested in knowing more, read the review below.

Radial Root Cyclone Technology

The Dyson DC41 is composed of Radial Root Cyclone Technology, which you might have encountered with other Dyson innovations. This smart technology changes the way that airflows through your vacuum cleaner, so that suction power can be maximized.

Additionally, the bagless vacuum cleaner has an active base plate, which allows for optimized cleaner head contact so that air leaks can be reduced and suction power can be enhanced.

This technology therefore ensures that the DC41 Animal has extremely strong suction at the cleaner head for efficient vacuuming. Additionally, this technology provides 20% more brush bar power for powerful suction on all floor surfaces.

Self-Adjusting Head

Another great feature of the Dyson DC41 is its cleaner head, which self-adjusts depending on the floor type. This cleaner head does not require dials or knobs to adjust, ensuring that, your vacuuming is less labor intensive.

The reason that the cleaner head adjusts is to ensure optimized floor contact, so that all pollen, bacteria, and mold in your house can be captured.

Convenient Attachments

The Dyson DC41 also comes with convenient attachments, such as a tangle-free turbine tool. This tool makes use of counter rotating heads with brushes, so that hair and dirt can be removed without tanglingup in the vacuum.

The vacuum cleaner also has an instant release wand which stretches up to 5 times in length; so that stairs and high reach areas can be cleaned with ease.

Easy to use

The DC41 is also simple to use, with several design features that are considered user friendly. It has a motorized brush bar, which can be turned off when cleaning delicate floors, hard floors, and rugs.

The vacuum cleaner also has a quick release cable, and a sturdy and clear bin that will not break.

Dyson Ball Technology

Last but not least, the Dyson DC41 has Dyson Ball Technology, which gives it a streamlined profile for simple steering around your home. This technology consists of a Dyson ball located at the base of the vacuum, which gives you the ability to tilt the DC41 from side to side as you turn.

This technology therefore ensures that you can easily maneuver the DC41 close to hard to reach places and edges.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Are the wand and hose of good quality?

A. Yes, these two components are of excellent quality. The instant high reach wand and the hose act smoothly, so that you can reach high and difficult to clean areas.

Q. Is the Dyson DC41 a noisy vacuum?

A. The DC41 Animal bagless emits the normal amount of noise, though you might find that is relatively quiet when compared to other vacuum cleaners.

Final Verdict

As you can see, the DC41 exemplifies everything that is great about Dyson. This Animal bagless vacuum cleaner uses its streamlined design and rich features to efficiently clean all corners of your home, so that it can remain in tip top shape at all times. What more can you ask for?
