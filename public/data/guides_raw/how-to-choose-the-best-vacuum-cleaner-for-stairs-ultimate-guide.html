How To Choose The Best Vacuum Cleaner For Stairs [Ultimate Guide]

Have you ever attempted to clean your stairs with a standard vacuum cleaner?
If yes, then you know how difficult and frustrating it is to try and pick up pieces of dirt along the corners, with a heavy appliance weighing you down.
What is the solution?
How exactly are you supposed to keep your stairs clean?
Several vacuum companies have seen the plight of many homeowners, and they have therefore decided to design vacuum cleaners which can be used on the stairs.
So if you have carpeted or non-carpeted stairs which you struggle to clean, then this informative guide will help you pick out the best stairs vacuum cleaner.

Factors to Consider when Choosing the Best Vacuum Cleaners for Stairs

There are a range of vacuum options for your stairs, each offering different functions and features. That is why below I have made a list of the main factors that you need to consider when you go shopping, so that you find the best vacuum cleaner for your stairs.

Weight

Standard vacuum cleaners are too bulky to carry up and down the stairs. It is for this reason that you need to identify a vacuum cleaner which is lightweight, so that your cleaning can be made much easier. A lightweight vacuum cleaner is easy to carry and easy to maneuver, in addition to being easy to transport from one part of your home to another.
This also guarantees that you will not feel fatigued when you are cleaning your stairs.

Pivoting Head

Stairs have tight corners, which are quite difficult to clean. However, if your vacuum cleaner has a pivoting head you can easily get to those tight corners and narrow areas. In this way there will be no dirt left behind. There are vacuum heads of varying pivoting degrees, and a 180 degree rotation will be suitable for your stair cleaning.

Attachments

Attachments are simply extra tools that can be integrated into your vacuum, so that you can have increased versatility in your cleaning. The attachments you should look for include crevice tools for hard to reach areas, and deep cleaning brushes for efficient carpet cleaning. Having helpful attachments will make your stairs cleaning quicker, easier, and more efficient.

Cordless vs Corded

When selecting a vacuum cleaner for your stairs, you will need to choose between a corded vacuum and a cordless vacuum. Corded vacuum cleaners are good when you need to clean for a long period of time, which is not the case when you are cleaning your stairs.
Therefore, a cordless vacuum cleaner will be best for cleaning your stairs. My advice would be to go with a rechargeable handheld vacuum, with a powerful lithium ion battery. Also look for a cordless vacuum cleaner with fade free power, as this allows for optimum power efficiency.

Types

There are 3 main types of vacuum cleaners that you can use for your stairs, including upright vacuums, canister vacuums, and handheld vacuums. An upright vacuum provides great power and versatile use on different surfaces. If you want an upright model to vacuum your stairs, choose one with a detachable canister that will make the process easier for you.
When it comes to canister vacuums go with one with a collapsible wand, so that it can be easier to use on the stairs. However, my recommendation would be for you to go with a handheld vacuum, as this works best on the stairs. Unfortunately, handheld vacuum cleaners are not versatile, and will not work well on your carpeted floors.

Bagged vs Bagless

Another big decision that you need to make is bagged or bagless. As you can deduce from the name, a bagged vacuum cleaner uses a dust bag, while a bagless vacuum cleaner does not use a dust bag.
Bagged vacuum cleaners are good because they capture dust in the air, which is beneficial to those with allergies. However, as bagged vacuum cleaners fill up, they negatively affect suction power. Bagged vacuum cleaners also require you to buy bags regularly, which is expensive. If you do go with a bagged vacuum cleaner, go with one that is self-sealing.
On the other hand we have bagless vacuum cleaners, which do not require you to buy bags on a regular basis. However, bagless cleaners will send dust back into the air if you do not empty them carefully, and they also call for constant maintenance of the filters.

Noise

No one enjoys noise, including especially when it is produced by a vacuum cleaner. It is therefore important for you to look for a vacuum that will not be unbearably loud as you clean. The amount of noise that is acceptable is in the 70-77dB range, however, if you want a super quiet cleaner go down to the mid 60s decibel range.
Your cleaning can now be comfortable and quiet.

Filtration System

A filtration system is an important part of any vacuum cleaner, as it determines how efficient your vacuum cleaner is at picking up dirt and debris from your stairs. My advice would be to look for a HEPA filter, as it can filter out tiny dirt particles, pet hair, and dust, with up to 99.97% efficiency. This strong filtration will also remove dirt from the air.

Storage

Last but not least, we have storage. Once you have finished cleaning the stairs, you will need to store your vacuum cleaner. However, if you buy a large and bulky vacuum cleaner, it will be difficult for you to store it. You should therefore look for a compact vacuum cleaner which will be easy to store in your home.
P.S. This point is mostly useful to those who do not have a lot of space in their homes.

Conclusion

Using the factors detailed above, you can now narrow down the best vacuum cleaner for your stairs. The factors given above are simple and easy to follow, and will ensure that you make the best decision when you go out shopping. Best of luck to you!
