Detailed Review & Features Breakdown

Comprehensive examination of the V6 Trigger (same as Dyson DC58 Handheld) features, performance, and ergonomics.

Powerful Performance

When it comes to handheld vacuum cleaners, many people complain that they lack power. That is why Dyson has opted to integrate the Dyson digital motor V6 in this vacuum cleaner, so that you can benefit from a powerful performance. This motor spins up to 110,000 times per minute, which is nearly 3 times faster than the standard motor. This digital motor makes the V6 Trigger the most powerful handheld vacuum cleaner on the market.

Furthermore, the V6 Triggers allows for fade-free power, ensuring that you can maintain powerful suction throughout your cleaning. When it comes to recharging your battery, the Dyson V6 Trigger will recharge 3 times faster than its competitors.

There is also a boost mode option, which gives you the opportunity to vacuum at a much higher suction power.

Great Accessories

When it comes to the accessories of the Dyson V6 Trigger, you will be happy with the mini motorized tool which is used for cleaning small spaces and upholstery.

Additionally, the V6 Trigger has a combination accessory tool, which can be transformed into a crevice tool for cleaning tight spots, and a brush tool for spot cleaning and dusting. You can now use the Dyson V6 Trigger around your home, in your car, and on your boat.

Patented Technology

Like other Dyson handheld vacuum cleaners, the V6 Trigger makes use of patented Dyson cyclone technology. More specifically, this vacuum has been engineered with 2 Tier Radial cyclones, which complement the digital motor. The technology works by spinning air at rapid speeds, while the cyclones inside the vacuum cleaner create centrifugal forces that are thousands of times more forceful than gravity.

In this way pet hair, dirt, and dust will be tossed from the airflow and directly into the bin.

Easy to Handle

Lastly, the Dyson V6 Trigger is incredibly easy to handle and maneuver, as it has been built with an ergonomic design that fits comfortably in your hand. This natural fit means that you can clean without fuss, and without feeling fatigued.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions answered about the Dyson V6 Trigger (same as Dyson DC58 Handheld) Review.

Q: Can the Dyson V6 Trigger be used when charging?
No. When you are recharging this vacuum cleaner, you cannot operate it. Thankfully the recharge time is short, so that you can quickly go back to cleaning your house.
Q: How heavy is the Dyson V6 Trigger?
This vacuum cleaner is incredibly lightweight, and comes in at just under 3lbs.
Q: How long is the battery life of this vacuum cleaner?
In the low power mode it is between 15 and 20 minutes, and in the high power mode (boost mode) it s between 5 and 7 minutes.

Final Verdict

Our summary recommendation

When it comes to handheld vacuum cleaners, the Dyson V6 Trigger presents itself as an excellent option. This vacuum cleaner is powerful, lightweight, and comfortable to maneuver, which makes it simple to clean hard to reach areas. Despite the lack of a mounting bracket, the V6 Trigger is still a great vacuum to own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert guidance for selecting the perfect vacuum cleaner

How does VacCompare evaluate vacuum cleaners?

VacCompare analyzes manufacturer benchmarks and verified specifications including suction pressure (kPa), motor airflow wattage, acoustic noise emissions (dB), dust container capacity, sealed HEPA filtration percentage, and battery runtime.

What suction pressure (kPa) is needed for deep carpets?

For high-pile carpets and heavy pet hair, we recommend a vacuum cleaner with at least 18-22 kPa of suction power paired with a motorized brush roller.

Why is sealed HEPA filtration important for allergies?

True HEPA filtration traps 99.97% of fine particles down to 0.3 microns, including dust mites, pollen, dander, and mold spores. A fully sealed system ensures air does not escape around the filter.
